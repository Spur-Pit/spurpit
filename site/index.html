Contract Guarantees · SpurPit.sol

Four fight modes. Zero human referees.

01 · GRADUATION

Curve Escape

Side A wins by instant KO the exact block curve.graduated() flips true before the bell. If it fails to graduate by resolveAt, Side B takes the pot.

02 · CURVE LINE

Reserve Over / Under

Reads curve.realQuoteReserve() in ETH directly from the Pons V2 bonding curve at the bell (or settles immediately for OVER if the token graduates early).

03 · CLASH

Token vs Token

Pit two live Pons launches head-to-head. First to graduate wins by instant KO; otherwise the higher realQuoteReserve() at the bell takes the ETH pot.

04 · HAND CHECK

Whale / Dev Accountability

Snapshots the token balance of the deployer or any named whale at opening. If even 1 wei of tokens leaves before the bell, DUMP wins by instant KO.
